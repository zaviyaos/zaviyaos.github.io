# Known issues

## All devices

- Desktop Mode asks once per session before the right stick can move the
  mouse pointer. Select **Allow** and it works until the next restart.

## KONKR Pocket FIT Elite

- A charge limit below 100% can make the battery report "Over voltage" a few
  minutes after the limit is reached, and charging stops. Unplugging the
  charger and restarting clears it. Keep the limit at 100% on this model.

## AYN Odin 2 Portal

- Units built with the second panel type show a black screen.

## Retroid Pocket 6

- The internal microphone does not work yet.

## AYANEO Pocket S 1K / S 2K

- The AYANEO button isn't mapped yet.
- Touch is off on some units.

!!! note

    Not listed here? [Open an issue](https://github.com/hashtagbasit/Zaviya/issues)
    with the device model and the [debug logs](troubleshooting.md#debug-logs).
