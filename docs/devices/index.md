# Supported Devices

One image per chip. You pick your device in the OpenABL menu on first boot and
it sets itself up. Pick a maker below for its devices, chip and notes.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   [**AYN**](ayn.md)

-   [**KONKR**](konkr.md)

    Pocket FIT and Pocket FIT Elite

-   [**MANGMI**](mangmi.md)

    Air Y Pro and Pocket Max (Beta)

-   [**Retroid**](retroid.md)

-   [**Lenovo**](lenovo.md) · In testing

    Legion Y700 Gen 3, Gen 4 and Gen 5

-   [**REDMAGIC**](redmagic.md)

    REDMAGIC 6, built from source

The 865 devices are marked Beta in the Tested column. A blank cell means nobody
has reported on that device here yet. If you run Zaviya on one,
[tell us how it runs](https://github.com/hashtagbasit/SteamOS-ARM-Port/issues),
even if everything works. Images are on [Downloads](../downloads.md).
